# Kekenik Pixel — Manual Verification Checklist (`docs/MANUAL_TEST.md`)

Follow these **55 plain-language click-by-click steps** to visually inspect and verify all of Agent 3's deliverables (Layers, Groups, Frames, Cels, Timeline, Animation Playback, Onion Skin, Tags, Slices, Tilemaps & Tilesets) in the running application (`npm run dev` or `npm run dev:desktop`):

---

## Part 1: Feature Center & Demo Documents Verification (Steps 1–8)
1. **Launch Kekenik Pixel**: Start the app (`npm run dev` or `npm run dev:desktop`). Confirm the top Menu Bar, Document Tabs, Context Bar, Right ToolBar, Left Color Bar, Floating Preview Window, and Bottom Timeline load without errors.
2. **Open Feature Center (`F1`)**: Click **`Feature Center (261)`** in the top-right Menu Bar (or `Help > Feature Center...`). Confirm it displays **141 Tested Features** (21 Agent 1 + 60 Agent 2 + 60 Agent 3).
3. **Run All 60 Agent-3 Self-Tests**: Click the **`Run Agent 3 (60)`** button in the Feature Center. Confirm all 60 Agent-3 features turn green with **`PASS`** and millisecond timings.
4. **Run All 141 Tested Features**: Click **`Run All Tested (141/141 PASS)`** and confirm every single feature across Agents 1, 2, and 3 passes.
5. **Filter by Agent 3 & Test "Show me"**: Click the **`Agent 3`** filter button in the Feature Center, find `onion.red_blue_tint`, and click **`Show me`**. Confirm it opens the `onion_skin_lab.ase` demo document with Onion Skin enabled.
6. **Open Walk Cycle Demo via File Menu**: Click **`File > Open Demo: walk_cycle.ase (Agent 3 Walk & Tags)`**. Confirm an 8-frame ranger walk & slash animation opens with 4 cascading tags (`idle`, `walk`, `step_sub`, `attack`).
7. **Open Layer Groups Demo via File Menu**: Click **`File > Open Demo: layer_groups_blends.ase (Agent 3 Groups)`**. Confirm nested Group Layers (`Environment Group`, `Magic Lighting Group`), a `REF` Reference Guide layer, and Z-index badges (`+1`, `-1`) appear in the Timeline.
8. **Open Tilemap & Slices Demos via Home Tab**: Click the **`Home`** tab and click **`5. tilemap_dungeon.ase`** and **`6. slices_9patch_ui.ase`**, confirming both open in document tabs.

## Part 2: Layers, Groups, Reference Layers & Blend Modes (Steps 9–19)
9. **Switch to `layer_groups_blends.ase`**: Click the `layer_groups_blends.ase` tab. Look at the left column of the Timeline and confirm nested layers are indented under `Environment Group` and `Magic Lighting Group` with colored swatch bars.
10. **Collapse & Expand a Layer Group**: Click the **`▾`** chevron next to `Magic Lighting Group` in the Timeline. Confirm its child layers (`Floating Crystal`, `Halo Aura`) collapse; click **`▸`** to expand them again.
11. **Recursive Group Visibility**: Click the **Eye** icon on `Magic Lighting Group`. Confirm both child layers inside the group hide on the canvas at once; click the Eye icon again to show them.
12. **Isolate / Solo a Layer (`Alt+Click Eye`)**: Hold **`Alt`** and click the **Eye** icon on `Floating Crystal` (or click `Layer > Isolate Layer (Solo)`). Confirm all other unrelated layers hide so you can inspect `Floating Crystal` solo; `Alt+Click` again to restore visibility.
13. **Multi-Select Layers (`Shift+Click` / `Ctrl+Click`)**: Hold **`Ctrl`** (or `Shift`) and click two layer rows in the Timeline. Click `Layer > Group Selected Layers` to place them into a new folder, then press `Ctrl+Z` to undo.
14. **Drag-and-Drop Layer Reorder & Regroup**: Drag a layer row up or down in the Timeline left column and drop it onto another layer or group folder. Confirm the layer stack order updates on the canvas and can be undone with `Ctrl+Z`.
15. **Inline Rename Layer (`F2`)**: Select any layer in the Timeline, right-click it, and click **`Rename Layer (F2)`** (or press `F2`). Type `Hero Armor` and press `Enter`.
16. **Layer Properties Dialog (`Shift+P`)**: Double-click a layer name in the Timeline (or press `Shift+P` / `Layer > Layer Properties...`). Change **Blend Mode** to `multiply`, adjust **Opacity**, enable **Custom Timeline Row Color**, pick a color, and click **OK**.
17. **Add a Reference Guide Layer**: Click **`Layer > New Reference Layer`**. Confirm a new layer with a purple **`REF`** badge is added with a guide underlay (visible in the editor, excluded from exports).
18. **Duplicate & Merge Down Layer**: Click **`Layer > Duplicate Layer`**, then click **`Layer > Merge Down`**. Confirm the duplicated layer merges cleanly into the layer below it.
19. **Flatten Visible & Convert To/From Background**: Click **`Layer > Flatten Visible`**, verify the visible layers flatten into one `Flattened` layer, press `Ctrl+Z` to undo, and test **`Layer > Convert To / From Background`**.

## Part 3: Frames, Cels, Linked Cels, Z-Index & Timeline Matrix (Steps 20–30)
20. **Switch to `walk_cycle.ase`**: Select the `walk_cycle.ase` tab. Observe the 8 frames (`1..8`) and the continuous linked cel pill bar across all 8 frames on the `Ground Track` layer.
21. **Add New Frame vs New Empty Frame (`Alt+B`)**: On `Ground Track` (which has Continuous / Prefer Linked Cels enabled), click **`+F`** (`Frame > New Frame`) and confirm the new cel automatically links to the previous cel. Then click **`Frame > New Empty Frame (Alt+B)`** and confirm it creates an unlinked blank cel. Press `Ctrl+Z` twice.
22. **Select a Rectangular Range of Cels (`Shift+Click`)**: Click the cel at Layer `Ranger Body`, Frame `2`, then hold **`Shift`** and click Layer `Blade Arc FX`, Frame `5`. Confirm the 2×4 rectangular block of cels highlights in blue.
23. **Drag Cels to Move/Swap, Copy (`Ctrl`), and Link (`Alt`)**: Drag a cel in the Timeline matrix to another frame slot to move/swap it. Hold **`Ctrl`** while dragging a cel to copy it, and hold **`Alt`** while dragging across the same layer to link the cels.
24. **Link & Unlink Selected Cels**: Right-click a cel range in the Timeline and click **`Link Cels`**. Draw a stroke on one frame and confirm the edit appears on all linked frames. Right-click and select **`Unlink Cels`** to make them independent again.
25. **Quick Cel Opacity & Z-Index in Timeline Header**: Click a cel on `Ranger Body`. In the top-right of the Timeline header bar, drag the **`Cel Op`** slider and change the **`Z`** number field from `0` to `+2`. Confirm a `+2` badge appears on the cel in the matrix and changes its render order.
26. **Cel Properties Dialog**: Double-click any cel in the Timeline matrix (or `Frame > Cel Properties...`). Edit its Opacity, Z-Index, and X/Y Position offset, then click **OK**.
27. **Clear Cel Content (`Delete`)**: Right-click a cel and click **`Clear Cel Content`**. Confirm the pixel art clears while the frame column remains in the timeline, then press `Ctrl+Z` to restore it.
28. **Copy & Paste Cels via Timeline Clipboard**: Right-click a cel, click **`Copy Cel(s)`**, select another frame, right-click, and click **`Paste Cel(s)`**.
29. **Timeline Settings Popup (Thumbnails, Column Zoom, Dock Position)**: Click the **Gear (`⚙`)** icon in the Timeline header row. Change **Cel Thumbnails** from `Off` to `Small`, `Medium`, and `Large` to see live miniature pixel-art previews inside every cel cell! Drag the **Cel Column Width** slider to zoom the timeline width, and test docking the Timeline to `Left`, `Right`, and back to `Bottom`.
30. **Frame Properties (`P`), Constant Frame Rate & Reverse Frames**: Double-click frame header `1` (or press `P`) to open **Frame Properties**, set duration to `150 ms`, and click **OK**. Open **`Frame > Constant Frame Rate...`** to bulk-set durations, and click **`Frame > Reverse Frames`** to reverse the animation order.

## Part 4: Animation Playback, Onion Skin & Preview Window (Steps 31–40)
31. **Playback Transport & Speed Multiplier**: In `walk_cycle.ase`, click **Play (`▶`)** (or press `Enter`). Change the **Playback Speed** dropdown in the Timeline transport bar between `0.25x`, `0.5x`, `1x`, `2x`, and `4x` and observe the animation speed change live.
32. **Play Active Subtag vs Full Timeline**: Click the `walk` tag banner (Frames 3..6) in the Timeline header. Open the Timeline **Gear (`⚙`)** popup and toggle **`Play Active Tag / Subtag Only`** on and off to compare tag-only playback vs full-timeline playback.
33. **Timeline Embedded Mini-Player**: Look at the top-right corner of the Timeline header bar. Confirm the embedded **Mini-Player** displays a live animated preview and frame counter (`F/8`); click it to toggle Play/Pause.
34. **Status Bar Frame Input & `+` Button**: In the bottom-right Status Bar, type `5` into the `Frame:` field to jump to Frame 5, and click the **`+`** button next to it to append a frame.
35. **Enable Onion Skin (`F3`)**: Switch to the **`onion_skin_lab.ase`** tab (or press `F3`). Confirm you see Red-tinted previous ghost frames and Blue-tinted next ghost frames trailing and leading the active comet frame on the canvas, and red/blue highlights on the frame numbers in the Timeline header.
36. **Configure Onion Skin Popup & Dialog**: Click the **`▾`** caret next to the Onion Skin icon in the Timeline header (or `View > Onion Skin Settings...`). Adjust **Prev** (`1..8`) and **Next** (`1..8`) frame counts, drag **Initial Opacity** and **Falloff Step**, switch **Ghost Mode** between `Red / Blue Tint` and `Merge True Colors`, toggle **Current Layer Only**, and switch **Position** between `Behind Sprite` and `In Front of Sprite`.
37. **Floating Preview Panel Independent Playback**: Look at the floating **`Preview`** window. Click its **`Play / Pause`** button so the Preview window animates continuously even while you pause the main Timeline and paint on a single frame!
38. **Preview Tag Selector & 3×3 Tiled Preview**: In the top bar of the **`Preview`** window, select a specific tag (`Tag: walk`) from the dropdown, and click **`3x3`** to preview the animation tiled in a 3×3 grid.
39. **Preview Pan/Zoom Sync & Full-Screen (`Max`)**: Click **`Sync`** in the **`Preview`** window to display the dashed yellow viewport box, click inside the Preview canvas to recenter the main editor, and click **`Max`** (or press `F7`) to toggle maximized preview mode.
40. **Jump Between Tags (`T»` / `Alt+Right`)**: Click the **`T»`** button in the Timeline transport bar (or `Frame > Jump to Next Tag` / `Alt+Right`) to jump the playhead cleanly from tag to tag.

## Part 5: Tags, Slices & 9-Patch UI Workflow (Steps 41–47)
41. **Cascading Tag Banners & Drag Resize Handles**: In `walk_cycle.ase`, observe how the nested `step_sub` tag cascades onto a second row below `walk`. Click the **`[`** and **`]`** handle buttons on any Tag banner in the Timeline header to expand or shrink its frame span live.
42. **Tag Properties Dialog & Tags Panel**: Double-click any Tag banner (or click `Frame > Tags > Tag Properties...`) to edit its Name, From/To frames, Direction (`Forward`, `Reverse`, `Ping-pong`, `Ping-pong Reverse`), Repeat count (`0 = Infinite` or `1..N`), and Color. Also open **`View > Tags Panel`** in the right dock to play or manage all tags from a list.
43. **Switch to `slices_9patch_ui.ase`**: Click the `slices_9patch_ui.ase` tab. Confirm the Slice Tool (`Shift+C`) activates and the right dock opens the **`Slices`** panel showing `panel_9patch`, `play_button`, and `gem_badge`.
44. **Inspect 9-Patch Center Guides & Pivot Markers on Canvas**: On the canvas, confirm `panel_9patch` displays its solid outer slice box, dashed inner **9-patch scaling center rectangle**, and yellow circular **Pivot point** marker.
45. **Draw a New Slice & Drag to Move**: With the **Slice Tool (`Shift+C`)** active, click and drag on an empty area of the canvas to create a new Slice (`slice_4`). Then click and drag inside an existing slice on the canvas to reposition it.
46. **Animated Per-Frame Slice Keys**: In `slices_9patch_ui.ase`, switch between **Frame 1** and **Frame 2** in the Timeline. Watch the `play_button` slice bounds and pivot shift down by 2 pixels on Frame 2, demonstrating per-frame keyframed slices!
47. **Slice Properties Dialog, Select Slice Bounds & Export Slice**: Double-click a slice on the canvas (or click **`Edit`** in the Slices panel) to open **Slice Properties**. Toggle 9-Patch Center and Pivot coordinates. Click **`Select Slice Bounds`** to turn the slice rectangle into a selection mask on the canvas, and click **`Export`** in the Slices panel to open the cropped slice region as a brand-new sprite document tab!

## Part 6: Tilemaps & Tileset Editor Studio (Steps 48–55)
48. **Switch to `tilemap_dungeon.ase`**: Click the `tilemap_dungeon.ase` tab. Confirm the `Dungeon Tilemap` layer (marked with a blue **`TM`** badge in the Timeline) is active and the **`Tileset`** panel is open in the right dock showing `Tile #0 (Empty)` plus Tiles `#1..#4`.
49. **Paint Tiles in Tiles Mode (`Pencil`, `Eraser`, `Bucket`, `Rectangle`)**: In the **`Tileset`** panel, click **`Tile #4`** (Treasure Chest). Select the **Pencil Tool (`B`)** and click a floor cell on the canvas to place a treasure chest tile. Right-click (or use the **Eraser Tool (`E`)**) to clear a cell back to `Tile #0 (Empty)`. Select the **Paint Bucket Tool (`G`)** or **Rectangle Tool (`U`)** to flood-fill or draw rectangles of tiles!
50. **Test Tile Flip X, Flip Y & Rotate 90° Bitflags**: In the **`Tileset`** panel (or top Context Bar), select **`Tile #3`** (Corner Trim) and toggle **`FlipX`**, **`FlipY`**, and **`Rot90°`**. Click on the canvas to stamp flipped and rotated corner tiles.
51. **Highlight Used Tiles on Canvas**: In the **`Tileset`** panel, select **`Tile #3`** and click the **`Used (4)`** button. Confirm yellow highlight boxes appear on the canvas over every grid cell that uses Tile #3.
52. **Live Single-Tile Pixel Editor in Tileset Panel**: Look at the bottom of the **`Tileset`** panel under **`Edit Tile #N Pixels`**. Select **`Tile #1`** (Stone Wall) and left-click/drag inside the 96×96 tile editor box. Watch every single wall tile on the main dungeon canvas update simultaneously in real time! Press `Ctrl+Z` to undo.
53. **Test Pixels Mode (`Auto` / `Stack` / `Manual`) on the Canvas**: In the **`Tileset`** panel (or Context Bar), switch from `Mode: Tiles` to **`Mode: Pixels`** and set modification mode to **`Auto Tiles`**. Draw a new stroke with the Pencil tool across a tile cell on the main canvas — confirm a brand-new tile (`Tile #5`) is automatically created and added to the Tileset! Switch to **`Manual Tiles`** and draw on an existing tile cell to modify that shared tile in-place across all cells.
54. **Convert Regular Layer `<->` Tilemap Layer with Automatic Deduplication**: Click **`Layer > Convert Layer <-> Tilemap`** to convert a tilemap layer to a regular pixel layer, and click it again to automatically extract and deduplicate unique 16×16 tiles back into a Tileset!
55. **Add/Duplicate/Delete Tiles, Purge Unused & Tileset Properties**: In the **`Tileset`** panel, click **`+ Tile`** and **`Dup`**, then click **`Purge Unused`** to automatically remove unreferenced tiles and remap tile indices. Finally, click **`Props`** to open the **Tileset Properties** dialog and inspect Tile Size, Origin X/Y, Base Index, and Export Columns.
